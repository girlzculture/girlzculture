Status: **AUTOMATED ONLY; not released, hosted or real-business acceptance.** Continues the agreed software-first marketing clauses (451, 653–664 and related SF-MARKETING wiring) in PR #80. The existing discount calculation, payment/deposit rules, provider settings and both $25 AI caps are unchanged.

## Implemented

- Promotions now exposes an owner-only marketing workspace using the authenticated business's saved portfolio photos (including before/after classifications), current services, eligible promotions and completed service references. Customer identities and private client attachments are excluded from the source projection. A selected foreign media/service/promotion/booking ID is rejected before draft construction; the request cannot supply a business ID.
- Deterministic EN/FR/ES/Simplified Chinese draft templates preserve actual names, prices and protected-deposit terms. They do not invent image analysis, customer demand, reviews or outcomes and make no extra provider call. All four copies are editable and separately reviewed. This verifies the template/record path, not native-speaker copy quality or AI-provider generation.
- The server derives the actual canonical public business path and booking destination with the selected service/promotion IDs. Caption text cannot substitute an arbitrary URL. Saved drafts retain their selected source snapshot, revision and audit history.
- Save requires exact persisted readback. Publication requires the owner, exact saved revision, review of all four language versions and an explicit declaration that selected work may be published. Scheduling uses the business time zone, explicit expiry and the existing offer window. Editing revokes approval; uncertain save/approval retries retain their operation identity. Errors keep entered work.
- The supported automatic publication destination is the existing Girlz Culture public business page, within a small Business updates subsection. The existing public layout remains intact. A protected worker runs every five minutes only on a published Production deployment; it rechecks current sources, owner identity and page eligibility. Cancelled, expired, changed-source, transferred or disabled-owner content fails closed in the public read before the worker catches up. The worker records expired/needs-review transitions.
- Manual social draft export contains the reviewed language, tags and actual booking link and explicitly says it has not been posted externally. No social account, email/SMS channel or notification recipient was invented, connected or contacted.
