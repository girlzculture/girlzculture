## Demonstrated gap and correction

`assistant-manual-sale-before.log` reproduced `ASSISTANT_UNKNOWN_TOOL`: the planner explicitly sent every financial action to Finances, despite the agreed received-payment example. New `get_manual_sale_options` exposes only currently permitted own-business service/professional identifiers and names. `prepare_manual_service_sale` prepares a risk4 review with exact received cents, method, time, professional and optional client name. It cannot supply a business scope, customer account, provider charge, refund, payout, deposit change or booking.

Ambiguous walk-in requests must clarify appointment versus completed sale, receipt status/method/amount and whether a named person is the professional or client. Anonymous legitimate sales are allowed. An explicit UI confirmation records one sale/full receipt through the existing `record_business_finance` RPC and its canonical idempotency, compensation and audit logic. No new ledger or payment integration exists. Unknown costs stay null.

The confirmation wrapper preserves all established assistant actions. For this action it locks business/identity/membership/proposal, freshly checks the exact granted finance_log or finance_manage permission and professional assignment, compares canonical current service/professional/time-zone payloads, and reads the actual persisted sale and full receipt. A retry reads the durable records again; missing receipt, changed scope/source or revoked permission cannot replay a successful audit result. Source/model/tool queries remain own-business only. Both AI caps, memory policy and provider configuration are unchanged.
