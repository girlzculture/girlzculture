## Evidence boundary

ADVISE-02 and FIN-19 require defensible costs before profitability advice. Canonical booking-derived service sales do not currently have recorded direct costs, and expenses and wage obligations previously had no per-service allocation. A service's gross recorded value therefore cannot establish its profit. Manual sales without a canonical service identity are also excluded; names never join records.

The new Finances panel reports **owner-reviewed recorded contribution**, not net profit, cash received, platform-verified complete costs, spare capacity or forecast demand. It compares completed appointment counts over two equal local-calendar-day periods. A positive contribution with fewer completed appointments is a reason to review the own service and current calendar; it does not establish that the service is underbooked relative to capacity. The full profitable-but-underbooked advice clause is **not marked complete** by this narrower implementation.

Only completed reporting periods are eligible. The default report ending today provides a direct choice of the previous 28 completed days instead of issuing an invalid request. The current and comparison dates are visible. Recorded completion dates take precedence over scheduled dates where present; current completion status and verified refund evidence are evaluated as of the read.

## Canonical cost review and safety

Owners allocate existing own-business operating expenses and wage obligations to a service period. They explicitly declare that applicable materials, labor and overhead have been reviewed without double counting. An empty allocation requires a separate explicit zero declaration. This declaration is owner-recorded evidence; the platform does not verify that all real-world costs have been entered.

The result subtracts recorded refunds, the booking's saved commission, recorded direct costs and the reviewed allocations from recorded completed-service value. Missing or stale reviews, booth-rental service turnover and unverified payment evidence cannot produce positive contribution advice. Sources and totals are bounded. Allocations across current reviews cannot exceed their canonical source amounts. Inventory-asset purchases are not treated as operating expenses. No allocation creates or edits a receipt, expense, wage, subscription or charge.

The private review table uses RLS, service-role-only invoker RPCs, current permission checks and owner-only writes. Reads require the fresh intersection of earnings, bookings and styles access with full-business finance scope. Raw finance records, client facts, source identities and review notes are not exposed through a model summary. SQL uses canonical operation audit/idempotency, exact source fingerprints and revisions; it rechecks the fingerprint after acquiring source locks. The server reports success only after a separate fresh authoritative read returns the expected review revision and fingerprint. Changed evidence fails closed.

Lost responses retain the exact original request for an explicit retry. A source-conflict refresh preserves the editable draft, replaces its evidence binding and requires a new explicit completeness declaration before a fresh save. A removed source remains visibly unresolved and blocks saving until the owner removes that allocation; it is never silently dropped. Refresh failure clears prior recommendations and saved-result claims. Business or period changes create a new bound workspace.

The assistant's existing earnings and business summary reads now receive a bounded service-contribution projection for the exact requested completed local-calendar period. Up to two own-service names, measured counts, contribution values and action links are included with total qualifying counts and an excerpt flag. Client identities, review notes, source IDs and allocations are excluded. Partial-day/current-day periods remain explicitly unavailable rather than being silently widened. The canonical reader's returned dates and timezone must match the request binding. Both summary tools refresh prior evidence; revoked earnings, bookings or styles access removes contribution evidence and the transcript derived from it before model input. No tool vocabulary, provider, financial write or cost cap changed.
