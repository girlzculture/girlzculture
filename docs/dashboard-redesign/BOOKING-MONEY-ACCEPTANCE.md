# Booking cohort money explanations

Status: **AUTOMATED ONLY**, not production acceptance. This read-only extension uses the existing operating books, verified platform receipt/refund projection and explicit business-recorded receipts. It creates no ledger, charge, provider request, migration or financial write.

## Meaning and scope

- The cohort contains this business's non-test appointments dated within the selected inclusive dates in its configured time zone. Completion/receipt dates do not move a booking into another appointment cohort. Cancellation and no-show counts are distinct. States are **current recorded states**, not reconstructed historical states at the reporting cutoff.
- Agreed value is the booked price, not measured lost revenue. Verified platform receipts/refunds are separate from manually recorded business receipts/refunds. Linked receipts before the appointment period are included through its end date; later receipts/refunds are excluded. The resulting net recorded retained receipt amount is neither bank settlement nor a refund-policy decision. A claimed paid/refunded provider record lacking canonical verification fails closed.
- Promotion usage requires a matching booked business-offer ID and its saved title/price/discount snapshot. A deleted offer can retain its valid immutable booked snapshot. Contradictory or incomplete attribution suppresses campaign aggregates and exposes an incomplete-evidence count; it never becomes zero usage. Generic discount amounts or coupon codes do not invent business-offer attribution. No incremental revenue, conversion lift, profitability or return on marketing is inferred.
- The route requires a freshly authenticated owner or both full `earnings` and `bookings` permissions. Canonical finance scope must be full-business. Database permission checks run before and after metadata reads. A professional's own earnings alone are insufficient. Selected metadata queries are constrained to the authenticated salon and already-authorized booking IDs, at most 100 IDs per chunk, 2,000 cohort bookings and 20,000 source records. A changed/missing/mixed record fails closed. A shorter period is required above the limits; no partial result is presented as complete.
- Raw books, customer/contact details and payment-provider IDs are never returned by this endpoint or sent to a model. Output contains aggregate counts/amounts, own saved campaign labels and explicit context only. No cross-business comparison or current/public campaign lookup occurs.
