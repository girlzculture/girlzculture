# New York location review

Checked against the linked official sources on 23 September 2026. This is an approval guide, not a statement that selecting a location type makes a business licensed. Only Hair & Braiding is open for onboarding. The other six categories remain waitlist-only.

## Hair, natural hair styling and braiding

Verify the practitioner's license covers the services offered and verify the business license. Renting a chair does not replace the area renter's business-license requirement. A residential business needs a separate business area, required facilities and inspection access. Remote practice requires a licensed fixed business location or active employment by its license holder; remote-service records must be retained at the licensed location for three years. Check the bond or insurance requirement separately. A “no insurance” application answer may be submitted for review; it is not evidence that the applicant satisfies the financial-security requirement. See [New York appearance enhancement law, Article 27 and 19 NYCRR 160.2–160.9](https://dos.ny.gov/appearance-enhancement-law) and the [business licensing page](https://dos.ny.gov/appearance-enhancement-business).

## Categories awaiting onboarding

| Category | Review required before future activation |
|---|---|
| Nail studio | Appropriate practitioner and appearance enhancement business licenses; residential/remote conditions above; nail-specific ventilation and employee financial-security requirements. [DOS business licensing](https://dos.ny.gov/appearance-enhancement-business). |
| Lash & Brow | Check each offered procedure against the licensed esthetics, cosmetology or waxing scope; a category label alone does not establish authorization. [DOS statutory definitions](https://dos.ny.gov/appearance-enhancement-law). |
| Aesthetics | Separate ordinary esthetics from medical procedures. Do not use an esthetics license to imply authority to perform laser skin rejuvenation, tattoo removal or other procedures identified as medicine by NYSED. [NYSED energy-device guidance](https://www.op.nysed.gov/sites/op/files/2025-02/Use%20of%20Energy%20Devices%20Including%20Lasers%20as%20the%20Practice%20of%20Medicine.pdf). |
| Massage & Wellness | Verify the massage therapist's New York license and current registration, permitted practice scope and applicable premises requirements. Appearance enhancement licensing does not authorize massage therapy. [NYSED consumer information](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/massage-therapy/consumer-information), [practice questions](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/massage-therapy/questions-answers). |
| Barbershop | Verify barber operator and shop owner/area renter licensing. Do not extrapolate the appearance enhancement remote-location rule to barbering or advertise a mobile barber shop as approved without regulator confirmation. [DOS shop licensing](https://dos.ny.gov/barber-shop-ownerarea-renter), [barber FAQ](https://dos.ny.gov/frequently-asked-questions-barber). |
| Tattoo | In New York City, verify the individual Department of Health tattoo license and required infection-control training; check local health and premises requirements for the actual location. Do not advertise home/mobile tattoo eligibility from a generic platform category. [NYC tattoo licensing](https://nyc-business.nyc.gov/nycbusiness/description/tattoo-license). |

## Approval record

The reviewer must record the actual location visit and evidence, check licenses against the offered services and address, and resolve applicable local premises/zoning requirements. A platform visit is distinct from a government inspection. The software blocks solo approval until the required actual visit is recorded; it cannot manufacture evidence or declare legal compliance. Keep home/mobile availability claims conditional on approval. No visits or government approvals were performed as part of this source review.
